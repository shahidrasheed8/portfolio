Overview

About This Project

Muzamil's Scoopery is a colorful, single-page landing page template for an ice cream and dessert shop. It showcases flavors, promotional offers, customer favorites, brand story, reviews, and a community gallery through a modern, fully responsive front-end design built for desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Highlights

Key Features

  1. Fully responsive single-page layout for desktop, tablet, and mobile
  2. Fixed glassmorphism navigation with active-section highlighting
  3. Animated hero section with product artwork and a customer rating card
  4. Infinite-loop flavor carousel with six product cards
  5. Promotional deals and customer favorites section
  6. Feature cards for ingredients, craftsmanship, delivery, and sustainability
  7. Brand story section with supporting imagery
  8. Horizontal customer reviews slider
  9. Community gallery with responsive image cards
  10. Front-end newsletter subscription form
  11. Smooth scrolling and scroll-to-top control
  12. Custom hover effects, transitions, scrollbar, and reduced-motion support
