Overview

About This Project

Job Hunt is a PHP OOP job portal that streamlines hiring for companies and job searching for candidates. Users can register, apply with screening questions, track application status, and manage profiles — while admins control listings, companies, and applicants from one panel.

Highlights

Key Features

  1. Candidate registration and login
  2. One-click job applications with screening questions
  3. Profile and CV management
  4. Real-time application status (pending, accepted, rejected)
  5. Company profiles and job listing management
  6. Admin panel for applicants, jobs, and companies
  7. Custom screening questions per job post
  8. Contact and informational pages
